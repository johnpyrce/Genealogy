A characterization of the recorded family collection along two dimensions — time, primarily by generation (many people carry no recorded dates, with recorded birth years overlaid), and lineage, the succession of main-line surnames, the branches that leave them, and the ancestry brought in by marriage — across four linked lenses. How to read this profile

Generations follow the alignment shared with the overview and atlas views: the largest connected family sets Gen 1; partners share a generation, parents one earlier, children one later. The 5 people with no established position count as Unaligned; no generation is invented for them.

Lineage segments are computed from the main-line families recorded in families.csv. Each segment begins at a main-line father who is not recorded as the child of a main-line family, and then follows main-line links: the partners of that segment’s main-line families and their children join the segment. This collection’s main line is not one descent but a succession of named lineages (for example Gościński, later Szost, Pyrc, Rams…), and the ribbon shows each segment across the generations it reaches. People recorded in collateral or spouse-ancestry families are not assigned to a segment; they appear in the grey “branches & spouse ancestry” band and count as “other” in the matrix.

Lens 3 adds the per-segment view: “offshoots” counts recorded collateral families that share at least one person with the segment; “ancestry traced” counts recorded spouse-ancestry families that share at least one person with the segment; the mini bars show the segment’s members per generation.

Lens 4 lists every recorded surname group with its generation span, the segments its people belong to, recorded spelling variants, and its role: a segment’s founding surname is its bearer; surnames whose people otherwise sit in main-line segments are partner surnames that joined the main line; the rest belong to branches and traced spouse ancestry.

Matrix cells count people per surname group and generation. A cell’s colour is the segment with the most people in that cell; hover for the exact breakdown. Birth-year decades count only recorded birth years; unknown dates contribute nothing and are never estimated.

Focus: clicking a ribbon band, generation column, matrix cell or row, or a Lens 3/4 row highlights that segment, surname, or generation across all lenses and dims the rest; the decade histogram recomputes for surname and segment focus. Click the same item again, press the Clear button, or press Escape to clear.

Gaps are first-class here: people without recorded birth years, unaligned people, and main-line fathers whose recorded parentage forms contradictory links. Contradictions are reported, not resolved.
