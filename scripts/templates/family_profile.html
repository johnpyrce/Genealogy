The shape of this family

A characterization of the recorded family collection along two dimensions — time, primarily by generation (many people carry no recorded dates, with recorded birth years overlaid), and lineage, the succession of main-line surnames, the branches that leave them, and the ancestry brought in by marriage — across four linked lenses. Click any segment, surname, or generation to highlight it across every lens. Nothing here estimates missing facts.

How to read this profile

Generations follow the alignment shared with the overview and atlas views: the largest connected family sets Gen 1; partners share a generation, parents one earlier, children one later. People without an established position appear under Unaligned.

Lineage segments are computed from the main-line families recorded in families.csv. Each segment begins at a main-line father who is not recorded as the child of a main-line family, and then follows main-line links: the partners of that segment’s main-line families and their children join the segment. This collection’s main line is not one descent but a succession of named lineages (for example Gościński, later Szost, Pyrc, Rams…), and the ribbon shows each segment across the generations it reaches. People recorded in collateral or spouse-ancestry families are not assigned to a segment; they appear in the grey “branches & spouse ancestry” band when their generation is established.

Lens 4 lists every recorded surname group with its generation span, the segments its people belong to, recorded spelling variants, and its role: a segment’s founding surname is its bearer; surnames whose people otherwise sit in main-line segments are partner surnames that joined the main line; the rest belong to branches and traced spouse ancestry.

Matrix cells count people per surname group and generation. Birth-year decades count only recorded birth years; unknown dates contribute nothing and are never estimated.

Focus: clicking a ribbon band, generation column, matrix cell or row, or a Lens 3/4 row highlights that segment, surname, or generation across all lenses and dims the rest; the decade histogram recomputes for surname and segment focus. Click the same item again, press the Clear button, or press Escape to clear.

Gaps are first-class here: people without recorded birth years, unaligned people, and main-line fathers whose recorded parentage forms contradictory links. Contradictions are reported, not resolved.
