Using the family tree

This viewer shows a focused family branch at a time. It is designed for finding a person, following their recorded relationships, and reading the source notes attached to their record.

Start exploring

Click a person card to make that person the focus. Drag across the diagram to pan, and use the mouse wheel, trackpad, or pinch gesture to zoom. Use Fit branch when the current branch no longer fits comfortably on screen, or Starting person to return to the initial view.

Find a person

Enter a name or exact person ID in Find a person, then choose a result. Search accepts Polish names with or without diacritics. Dates and IDs in the results help distinguish people who share a name.

Control what is shown

  • Generations expands the focused branch to two, four, or all recorded generations in each direction.
  • Source document limits the diagram and search results to relationships supported by a selected chart. The viewer keeps people with direct evidence from that chart available even when the chart does not record a connector for them.
  • Color cards by switches between recorded parent-role styling and normalized family-name styling. The legend explains the colors currently visible.
  • Show entire tree displays every connected group available under the current source selection. Use it when you need an overview rather than one person’s branch.

Read a person record

The selected-person panel lists the stable person ID, recorded birth and death years, source label, and registry notes. Blank values mean the registry does not record that fact; they do not establish that the fact was historically absent.

Alternative parent records

Some people have more than one recorded parent family. When this occurs, the viewer offers a parent-family selector. Selecting an option changes the displayed branch only. It does not choose between conflicting source accounts.

The chart is a view of the registry. It preserves unknown values, source notes, and alternate accounts rather than inferring missing relationships.
