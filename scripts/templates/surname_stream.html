How to read this chart

Generation positions reuse the family overview: partners share a level and each recorded parent–child link is one generation apart. The largest connected family supplies the reference. Disconnected people and components with contradictory constraints remain unaligned and are listed separately. Dates never determine placement.

The ribbons use a centered baseline. In People mode, one vertical scale is shared across generations and adjusts to the largest visible total. Widths are exact at the labeled generation positions; curves between positions are visual interpolation. Generations are discrete family steps, not equal spans of years. A continuous surname ribbon does not establish descent or a relationship between its members. “Other surnames” combines the remaining recorded groups; “Unknown” is the registry’s unknown surname group.

Birth ranges use recorded years only and may overlap. Missing birth or death years remain unknown; a missing death year does not establish that someone is living. Counts and generation positions are derived from the current registries.
