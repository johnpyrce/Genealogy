# Poland-USA Muszyna photo 2: green-name registry comparison

Source: `2026 - Poland-USA Muszyna - 2.pdf`. The PDF numbers below identify names printed in green in its table. Registry comparisons use `data/registries/people.csv` and `data/registries/families.csv`.

The **Family name** column gives the recorded father's surname followed by the recorded mother's maiden surname. **Unknown** means the registry does not document that parentage; no surname was inferred from the person's own name. Some `father_id` and `mother_id` slots in `families.csv` appear reversed, so the named parents and family notes were used to assign father and mother.
