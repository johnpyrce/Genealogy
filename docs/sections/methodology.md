# How to Read and Improve This Archive

## Sources and records

The supplied family charts are the starting point for this archive. Their images, transcriptions, and source-registry entries remain available so that each conclusion can be checked against the underlying material.

The editable CSV registries record people, family groups, source documents, and relationship evidence. The interactive viewers, text tree, GEDCOM export, and analytics are generated from those registries. A generated view is therefore a way to explore the research record, not an independent source.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Relationship evidence is recorded with one of three confidence levels:

- **Confirmed** for a relationship that the source clearly supports.
- **Probable** for a well-supported reading that remains open to revision.
- **Uncertain** for a provisional reading or placement.

Where sources give different parent families or accounts, the archive preserves each recorded alternative. Selecting a parent family in an interactive viewer changes the displayed branch; it does not resolve the historical disagreement. Blank dates, names, parents, or sources mean that the registry does not record that information.

## Using the archive responsibly

This is a family-research resource, not proof of identity or legal relationship. Surname similarity, a shared place, or a matching date can guide research, but a connection should be supported by a continuous record trail.

## Corrections and additions

For a correction or addition, [send feedback by email](mailto:rekarpyrce@gmail.com). Include the person or family ID when available, a clear description of the proposed change, and a citation or image of the supporting source. Explain whether the source confirms, makes probable, or leaves uncertain the proposed relationship.
