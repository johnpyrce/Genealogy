# Our Polish Family History

*The Gościński, Miczulski, Pyrć, Pyrce, and Rams families—from Muszyna and Wapienne, Poland, to America.*

This site began with six genealogy charts and photographs brought back from a family [visit](https://www.facebook.com/laura.hamman1/posts/pfbid02TGTPaaw2DR2X8yu7QA6yQJjqbnnXD1dopRw2sLet4iHDDB1hvQ2Eazn6PaAg5uRbl){target="_blank"} to Muszyna.
Follow [Sophie and Joseph Pyrce’s journey to America](/america/) and the changing spellings of their surname.
