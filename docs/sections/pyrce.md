# The Pyrce name

## The conventional surname history

**Pyrce** appears in surname references as a variant of **Peirce**, alongside the more familiar spellings **Pierce** and **Pearce**. Genealogist John Grenham’s [surname guide](https://www.johngrenham.com/findasurname.php?surname=Peirce) groups these spellings together.

The conventional history of this surname group leads back to **Piers**, a medieval French and English form of the given name **Peter**. The names became established in England, Wales, and Ireland. The [Dictionary of American Family Names, reproduced by Ancestry](https://www.ancestry.com/last-name-meaning/pierce/), describes this origin and notes regional differences in the use of Pierce and Pearce.

**That is not the history of the Polish family documented on this site.** Here, Pyrce is an American spelling of a name recorded earlier in Polish forms. The shared spelling does not establish a connection to the British or Irish surname families.

## This family’s name

For this family, **Pyrce** is the spelling used in American records. Earlier documents use several spellings, including **Pyrć**, **Pyrc**, **Puirch**, and **Pyrch**.
